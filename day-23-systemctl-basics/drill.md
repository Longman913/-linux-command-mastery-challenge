# Day 23 — Practice Drill

## Objective

Practice managing Linux services using systemctl.

## Service Used

The `cron` service was selected for the practical exercise.

## Activities Completed

- Checked the status of the cron service.
- Stopped the cron service.
- Confirmed that the service became inactive.
- Started the service again.
- Restarted the service.
- Confirmed that the service was active.
- Practiced reloading the service configuration.
- Disabled the service from automatic startup.
- Confirmed that the service was disabled.
- Enabled the service for automatic startup.
- Used `systemctl enable --now` to enable and start the service.
- Confirmed that the service was active.
- Confirmed that the service was enabled.

## Key Learning

I learned how systemctl is used to control services,
check their current state, and configure whether they
automatically start when the system boots.
