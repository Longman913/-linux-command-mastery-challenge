DAY 22 — CONTROLLING PROCESSES WITH SIGNALS

KILL
----
sleep 300 &
pgrep -a sleep
kill <PID>

KILL -9
--------
sleep 300 &
pgrep -a sleep
kill -9 <PID>

SIGHUP
------
sleep 300 &
kill -HUP <PID>

KILLALL
-------
sleep 300 &
sleep 300 &
killall sleep

PKILL
-----
sleep 300 &
pkill sleep

SUSPEND AND RESUME
------------------
sleep 300
Ctrl+Z
jobs
bg %1
jobs
fg %1
Ctrl+C

NOHUP
-----
nohup sleep 300 > nohup-output.txt 2>&1 &
jobs
pgrep -a sleep

DISOWN
------
sleep 300 &
jobs
disown %1
jobs

CLEANUP
-------
pgrep -a sleep

SUMMARY
-------
Day 22 demonstrated Linux signals, process termination, job
suspension, foreground/background control, nohup, and disown.
