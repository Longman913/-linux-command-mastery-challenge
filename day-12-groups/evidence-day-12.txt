=== Day 12 Groups & Access Circles Evidence ===

1. Created group:
sudo groupadd devs

2. Created users:
sudo useradd -m devuser1
sudo useradd -m devuser2

3. Added both users to devs:
sudo usermod -aG devs devuser1
sudo usermod -aG devs devuser2

4. Confirmed membership:
getent group devs

5. Removed devuser2:
sudo gpasswd -d devuser2 devs

6. Confirmed remaining membership:
getent group devs

7. Deleted the devs group:
sudo groupdel devs

8. Final verification:
getent group devs
